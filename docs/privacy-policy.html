Mona Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Touch Of Davinci Studios handles information in the Mona mobile application.

Effective date: June 3, 2026

1. Overview

Mona is designed to work primarily on-device. Core research and simulation features run locally on the phone without requiring an account or a remote server.

2. Information We Do Not Collect By Default

In its default standalone mode, Mona does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as precise location, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, text messages, call logs, payment data, or advertising identifiers.

This version of Mona also does not include built-in advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. Information You May Choose To Send

Mona includes optional backend-connected features, such as Turbo prediction and leaderboard submission. If you choose to use those features with a reachable backend, the app may transmit information that you enter or generate in the app, including:

  • recipe name
  • submitted by name or alias
  • selected cancer type
  • selected element values and recipe composition
  • prediction and leaderboard requests related to your app activity

4. How Network Connections Work

Mona does not rely on a Touch Of Davinci Studios cloud account for its core offline experience. Optional backend features are only used when the app is connected to a backend endpoint. If no backend is connected, those optional features do not send data anywhere.

If you connect Mona to a backend that is hosted or operated by you, your team, or another third party, that backend's own privacy and security practices will also apply.

5. How Information Is Used

Any information sent through optional backend features may be used to:

  • return Turbo prediction results
  • process leaderboard submissions
  • display community ranking data
  • support app functionality requested by the user

6. Sharing

Touch Of Davinci Studios does not sell personal information through Mona. Information may be shared only as needed to provide the optional backend functionality that a user chooses to connect and use.

7. Data Retention

Most core app usage stays on-device. If information is submitted to an optional backend or leaderboard, retention depends on how that backend is configured and operated.

8. Children's Privacy

Mona is not directed to children under 13, and Touch Of Davinci Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

9. Changes To This Policy

Touch Of Davinci Studios may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Mona, use the developer contact details listed in the Google Play store listing for Touch Of Davinci Studios.
